# Landing copy

This is the copy on the homepage and the two doors. It matches the product. Example data stays labelled. Nothing here claims a customer count, a logo, or a review that is not marked as an example.

## Homepage

Eyebrow: Marketplace

Title: Get expert work done by AI agents built by real experts, from $15, in minutes.

Line under the title: Turn what you know into an agent that earns while you sleep.

Doors:

- Get something done
- Earn from your skills

Honesty line: This demo writes the draft from the listing. It does not call a live model. No certification is claimed.

Shelf title: Jobs you can hire

Shelf note: Example listings are labelled. The price is the starting price for a draft in this demo.

Each card shows the price, the job name, “Draft in minutes,” the pitch, and an example rating when the rating is an example.

## Get something done

Step 1 title: What do you need done?

Promise, repeated: Get expert work done by AI agents built by real experts, from $15, in minutes.

Button: Show my matches

Step 2 title: Your top matches

Each match shows the price, “Draft in minutes,” the rating, a sample of the output, and one button: Pay {price} and get this.

Empty: No match yet. Try words like reply, checklist, drawing, scope, or bookkeeping.

After payment: Paid. Here is your draft.

Under that: Sample draft. Written from the listing in this demo. Not a live model and not certified work.

## Earn from your skills

Step 1 title: What are you great at?

Promise: Turn what you know into an agent that earns while you sleep.

Earnings: You keep 80% of the starting price when a buyer accepts the draft. Orbexa keeps 20%. This demo does not send a bank payout.

Button: Draft my listing

Step 2: a preview titled “What a buyer would see,” marked “Preview. Not a published listing yet.” The drafted title, pitch, instructions, category, and price can be edited. The account name, email, and password are on this screen if the person is new.

Button: Publish

After publish: You are live. Buyers pay {price} and get a draft in minutes. You keep 80% when they accept. This demo does not send a bank payout.

## Footer

This demo writes each draft from the listing. No card is charged unless a Stripe test key is set. Live Stripe keys are refused. No certification is claimed.
