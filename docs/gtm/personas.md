# Personas

Six people, United States, phone at 390×844. Each script is the Playwright walk in `apps/web/e2e/personas.spec.ts`. A meaningful step is a decision that moves the job forward. Opening the homepage is not a step. The value has to be on the first screen before any of those steps.

## Buyers

### Maya Chen, bakery owner, Austin

Maya runs a six-person bakery. A catering client asked about a deposit and she wants a calm reply she can send, not a course in agents. She will pay about $15 if the sample sounds like a reply and the price is on the card.

1. From the homepage, choose **Get something done**.
2. Type: “Draft a calm support reply that restates the catering issue and the next step.” Choose **Show my matches**.
3. On the top three, choose **Support reply**, check the price ($15.00), the sample, and the example rating, then **Pay $15.00 and get this**.

She is done when the screen says the draft is paid and quotes her request.

### Derek Walsh, ops manager, Columbus

Derek runs operations for a 20-person HVAC company. New technicians start every month. He wants a checklist a lead tech can follow. He will pay about $25.

1. **Get something done**.
2. Type: “Turn a recurring operations task into a checklist a teammate can follow.”
3. Pay for **Ops checklist** at $25.00 and read the draft.

### Priya Shah, freelance brand designer, Brooklyn

Priya is tired of vague client emails. She wants the request turned into scope, assumptions, and what is out of scope, before she starts design. She will pay about $30.

1. **Get something done**.
2. Type: “Rewrite a vague request into scope, assumptions, and out-of-scope items for a client brief.”
3. Pay for **Scope note** at $30.00 and read the draft.

## Sellers

### Helen Ortiz, retired civil engineer, Tucson

Helen spent her career checking drawings. She wants that habit to earn while she is not at a desk. She will publish if the app drafts the listing, shows what a buyer would get, and tells her what she keeps.

1. From the homepage, choose **Earn from your skills**. The first screen states the seller promise and “You keep 80%.”
2. Answer “What are you great at?” with: “Checking engineering drawings for missing dimensions and clashes.” Choose **Draft my listing**.
3. Read the preview (title, pitch, instructions, $45, sample). Add her name, email, and password on that same screen. **Publish**.

She is done when the public listing says she is live and repeats the 80% share.

### Andre Brooks, bookkeeper, Atlanta

Andre closes the books at night for a handful of shops. He wants a listing that asks his usual receipt questions.

1. **Earn from your skills**.
2. “Closing the books for small shops and flagging expenses that need a receipt.”
3. Preview at $35, then publish.

### Luis Romero, rideshare driver, Phoenix

Luis drives days and coaches youth soccer on weekends. The skill he can sell is a one-hour practice a parent can lead at the park. He is the test that a seller does not need a credential, only a skill they can describe.

1. **Earn from your skills**.
2. “Writing youth soccer practice plans that parents can use at the park.”
3. Preview at $20, then publish.
